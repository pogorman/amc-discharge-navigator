The screens

Discharge Command Center

One screen: "Discharge Command Center, Contoso University Health · house-wide discharge and barrier view", with Ask Discharge Navigator and the app version on the right.

ElementMeaning
Ask Discharge NavigatorOpens a chat with the same agent as in Teams, as you, with three starter questions. Replies take about 15 to 30 seconds. New chat starts over. The dashboard reloads after each reply, because the agent can save drafts to a plan.
Unit tabsAll units, or one unit. Every tile, panel and row follows the selected unit.
Updated / RefreshWhen the data was loaded. Refresh reloads it from Dataverse, for example after someone closes a barrier in Teams.
KPI tilesExpected discharges today · Medically ready, still here (with avoidable bed-days) · Open discharge barriers · Prior-auth turnaround (median, approvals in the last 14 days) · High readmission risk (LACE ≥ 10). A red top edge means there's something to act on.
Open barriers by typeBarrier tasks not yet completed, grouped by task type.
Avoidable bed-days by unitDays since medically ready, for patients still in house.
Plans tablePatient, unit and bed, plan status, destination, expected discharge, avoidable days, open barriers, readmission risk. Sorted by avoidable days. Select a row for detail.
Plan drawerChart facts and the hospital course, then Agent drafts, then Tasks and barriers.
Agent draftsThe patient instructions (with a Not Started / Draft / Signed badge) and the prior-auth packet that Discharge Navigator saved to the plan. "Saved by Discharge Navigator. Draft for clinician review and signature." Reload pulls in a draft saved while the drawer is open.
Tasks and barriersOpen barriers have a red edge and a Barrier badge. Each task shows its status, owner role, assignee, external party, due date and notes. Mark complete closes a task (the app's only write).

Discharge Navigator in Teams or Microsoft 365 Copilot

A chat. It answers from live Dataverse through the Dataverse MCP server, with your permissions. The two drafting skills end with a Model: line that names the model deployment's model field, and every draft says it's for clinician review. When the agent offers to save a draft to the plan, say yes; that's what the Command Center shows.
